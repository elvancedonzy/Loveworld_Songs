TITLE: What A Life
URL: https://loveworldlyrics.com/song/what-a-life-2

VERSE 1

What a life we have in you
A life of joy, truth and holiness
sin brought condemnation
But has been repealed
By the shedding of your blood
The triune essence has
brought us peace
And has made us one in your household,
Oh Lord

CHORUS

Thank you, Lord, for choosing us
To be holy and without blame
Before you in love
Fulfilling your good pleasure

To be holy and without blame
Before you in love
Doing good works of righteousness
Most holy God

What a life of grace, glory, and love

Your love so graciously bestowed upon us

You are life and truth the absolute reality
That dispels all darkness and fear
